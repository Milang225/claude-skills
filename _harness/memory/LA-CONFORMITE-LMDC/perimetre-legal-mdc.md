Legal mapping done 2026-10-10 (full analysis: `/Users/bilefilsseverin/LA CONFORMITE LMDC/05_PLACE_DANS_L_ECOSYSTEME.md`).

- Reserved to expert-comptable inscrit (ord. 2009-387 art. 3, 5, 68, 70; décret 2012-868 art. 1): vérifier/apprécier/réviser/redresser les comptabilités (incl. journalisation, centralisation, comptes finaux, surveillance), audits comptables = certification contractuelle, titles creating confusion. Penalties 1–2 yrs + 0.5–5 M FCFA. Visa of états financiers: CGI art. 49 bis.
- CAC (AUSCGIE 695, 710, 712): only expert-comptables; controls conformity of the *comptabilité* only; SARL needs one only above 2 of 3 thresholds (bilan >125 M, CA >250 M, >50 salariés, art. 376).
- Conseil juridique (loi 96-672): consultations + rédaction d'actes pour autrui "en matière juridique" need agrément; fiscal scope unclear → MDC drafts no legal acts.
- Free zone recognised by texts: fiscal assistance/advice (loi uniforme LBC/FT 2023 art. 2-26 e → MDC is an AML-obliged entity; CGI art. 98 "conseils juridiques et fiscaux"), "conseil de son choix" in tax audits (LPF 15–16), preparing declarations with quarterly client list to DGI (LPF 62).
- CGA (décret 2002-146, art. 3 & 11): a "cabinet conseil" SARL can be CGA promoter with an expert-comptable holding ≥25 % capital who visas — planned for 2027.

**Why:** a commenter (Jojo Gwaam) claimed conformity is reserved to EC/CAC; MDC's earlier practice (comptabilité from scratch, reconstitution, écritures de régularisation, "visé et certifié conforme", "Auditeur & Réviseur") falls in the reserved zone.
**How to apply:** never produce bookkeeping, reconstitution, regularisation entries, états financiers or legal acts as MDC deliverables; route them to partners. The existing skills (mdc-expert §"2 TYPES DE MISSIONS", CONTRÔLEUR/visa sections) still describe these — flag them. Related: [[refondation-mdc-indice]].
