When the gérant of MDC asks for strategy or design, decide and build it fully ("ne me pose pas de questions, conçois comme si tu étais au contrôle de tout"; "conçois tout de A à Z", not necessarily reusing what exists).

**Why:** he feels lost among his own procedures and wants a finished, coherent system rather than choices to arbitrate.
**How to apply:** make the calls, state them as decisions, and flag only items that genuinely need an external check (lawyer, regulator). Don't end with "par lequel on commence ?". Related: [[refondation-mdc-indice]].
